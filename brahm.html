Vishnav Sampradayas

Vaishnavism centers on Lord Vishnu and his avatars like lord Rama, lord Krishna and lord narshimha :-
Vaishnav Sampraday refers to the various Hindu traditions that worship Vishnu as the supreme deity. There are four main Vaishnava Sampradayas (lineages or disciplic successions). These are the Sri Sampradaya, Brahma Sampradaya, Rudra Sampradaya, and Kumara Sampradaya. Each Sampradaya has its own lineage of teachers and specific philosophical viewpoints. Here's a breakdown of the four main Vaishnava Sampradayas:-

Sampraday Founder/Acharya worship God Philosophy
Madhwa Sampraday shri madhwacharya Lord krishna
Gaudiya Sampraday shri Ramanandacharya Lord krishna

• Madhwa Sampraday

Also known as the Ramanuja Sampradaya, it emphasizes the philosophy of Vishishtadvaita (qualified non-dualism). Their Acharya (spiritual teacher) is Bhagavati Sri Sita.

• Gaudiya Sampraday

Also known as the Madhvacharya Sampradaya, it follows the philosophy of Dvaita (dualism). Their Acharya is Brahma.
