Vishnav Sampradayas

Vaishnavism centers on Lord Vishnu and his avatars like lord Rama, lord Krishna and lord narshimha Vaishnav Sampraday refers to the various Hindu traditions that worship Vishnu as the supreme deity. There are four main Vaishnava Sampradayas (lineages or disciplic successions). These are the Sri Sampradaya, Brahma Sampradaya, Rudra Sampradaya, and Kumara Sampradaya. Each Sampradaya has its own lineage of teachers and specific philosophical viewpoints. Here's a breakdown of the four main Vaishnava Sampradayas:-

• Sri Sampraday

Also known as the Ramanuja Sampradaya, it emphasizes the philosophy of Vishishtadvaita (qualified non-dualism). Their Acharya (spiritual teacher) is Bhagavati Sri Sita.

• Brahm Sampraday

Also known as the Madhvacharya Sampradaya, it follows the philosophy of Dvaita (dualism). Their Acharya is Brahma.

• Rudra Sampraday

Also known as the Vallabha Sampradaya or Vishnu Swami Sampradaya, it follows the philosophy of Shuddhadvaita (pure non-dualism). Their Acharya is Bhagavan Shankar Ji.

• Sankadik Sampraday

Also known as the Nimbark Sampradaya, it follows the philosophy of Dvaitadvaita (dualistic non-dualism). Their Acharya is Sanakadik Muni.These four Sampradayas are considered to be the primary lineages through which Vaishnavism has been passed down through generations. They each have their own unique interpretations of scriptures, practices, and philosophies, while all ultimately worship Vishnu as the supreme being.
